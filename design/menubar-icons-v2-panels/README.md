# Switchboard panel + jack/plug explorations

Six follow-ups combining the rounded/split panel identity of v1 icons 11/15
with the larger open jack, solid plug, and arched cord of v1 icon 17.
All use the same cable geometry so the amount of panel detail can be compared.

- A: panel and cable only.
- B: adds one spare socket.
- C: adds two spare sockets.
- D: panel and cable with a divider.
- E: divider and one spare socket.
- F: divider and two spare sockets.

The canvas is 18 × 18 points. The frame is thinner than the cable, and the
divider stops short of it to avoid turning the crossing into a dark blob.
Preview boards show enlarged designs plus 18-point and 16-point light/dark samples,
exported at 2x. PNG assets are transparent at 1x, 2x, and 4x; SVG and PDF
masters preserve vector geometry. The generator checks stroked bounds for clipping.

Regenerate with `swift design/menubar-icons-v2-panels/generate.swift`.
These are preview assets and are not installed in the app.
